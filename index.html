WHAT I DID

End-to-end analytics workflow.

  • Cleaned and profiled order-level retail data, checking missing values, duplicates, dates, and numeric fields.
  • Analyzed sales, profit, orders, quantity, profit margin, products, categories, segments, regions, states, shipping modes, and discount bands.
  • Developed SQL queries using aggregations, joins, CASE statements, CTEs, and window functions.
  • Built Excel analysis and dashboard reporting using Pivot Tables, KPIs, and charts.
  • Created reusable Python analysis workflows for business-focused exploration.
  • Built interactive Power BI and Tableau dashboards covering KPIs, trends, profitability, regional performance, segments, discounts, and shipping metrics.
KEY INSIGHTS

What the data showed.

  • Technology generated the highest category sales.
  • West recorded the highest sales and profit.
  • Consumer was the strongest-performing segment.
  • Tables was the largest loss-making sub-category.
  • Bookcases and Supplies also generated negative profits.
  • Standard Class had the highest shipping activity.
BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Focus on high-performing Technology products, review pricing, discounts, and costs for loss-making sub-categories, maintain strong performance in the West region, and monitor profitability alongside sales growth.
