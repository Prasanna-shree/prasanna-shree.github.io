Superstore Sales

THE PROJECT

What I did.

  • Performed data quality checks and validated the dataset.
  • Analyzed sales and profitability across categories, regions, segments, and shipping modes.
  • Created Excel Pivot Table analysis and dashboard reporting.
  • Used SQL to explore business performance and trends.
  • Created a reusable Python analysis script.
  • Built dashboards in Power BI and Tableau.
  • Identified loss-making sub-categories and generated business recommendations.
KEY INSIGHTS

What the data showed.

  • Technology generated the highest category sales.
  • West recorded the highest sales and profit.
  • Consumer was the strongest-performing segment.
  • Tables was the largest loss-making sub-category.
  • Bookcases and Supplies also generated negative profits.
  • Standard Class had the highest shipping activity.
BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Focus on high-performing Technology products, review pricing, discounts, and costs for loss-making sub-categories, maintain strong performance in the West region, and monitor profitability alongside sales growth.
